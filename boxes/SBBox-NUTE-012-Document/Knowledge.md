# Knowledge — SBBox-NUTE-012-Document

Documents in NUTE RIS can represent registration dossiers, proposals, process regulations, progress reports, research results, council materials, policies and supporting evidence.

V5 treats a document as both:
- an immutable/original artifact used for provenance and human review; and
- an AI-readable text source when extraction succeeds.

The original artifact and extracted text must remain linked by identity and checksum so AI recommendations can be traced back to source material.
