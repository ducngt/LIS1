# Rules — SBBox-NUTE-012-Document

1. Original uploaded files must be retained as evidence/provenance; extracted text is derivative data only.
2. Extraction failure must never be silently interpreted as an empty or compliant document.
3. AI may analyze extracted content, but approval/rejection remains with authorized human roles and workflow contracts.
4. Every stored document must carry its original filename, MIME/type information, size, checksum, uploader identity and timestamp.
5. The Box depends on `binary-storage` and `document.text.extract` capabilities, never on a concrete filesystem, PDF library, office parser or cloud vendor.
